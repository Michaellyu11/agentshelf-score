02 How much do engines agree?

Not much. The average pairwise agreement is 44.8%. DeepSeek and Gemini are the most aligned pair at 57%. Claude and Perplexity agree the least at just 38%.

03 Which brands do AI engines favor?

Brand concentration is extreme. In each category, 2-3 brands dominate all recommendations while smaller brands are nearly invisible.

04 Does the same engine agree with itself?

No. Ask the same question 3 times, get different answers. Even the most consistent engine (Claude) changes its mind 32% of the time. Gemini is the most erratic at 52% variability.

Methodology

Engines: ChatGPT (GPT-5.4-mini), Gemini (2.5 Flash), Perplexity (Sonar), Claude (Haiku 4.5), DeepSeek (Chat)

Categories: Wireless earbuds, mechanical keyboards, running shoes, skincare

Queries: 15 per category × 4 categories = 60 unique queries, varying price range, use case, user persona, and specificity

Repetitions: 3 per (query, engine) pair — enough to observe patterns, not statistically rigorous at p<0.05

Brand extraction: Keyword matching against ~150 known brands with word-boundary checks

Cost: ~$40 total API spend

All data, code, and analysis: GitHub
